01 / Meet Justin

HUSBAND.
FATHER.
STILL BECOMING.

Just412 grew out of my roots as a Pittsburgh native and the way God changed my life fifteen years ago through the ministry of Adult & Teen Challenge. The number connects me to the place I call home, but it has also become a personal reminder of how I came to faith in Jesus Christ.

By the time I entered the program, I had been using and selling drugs since I was thirteen. My life had become consumed by heroin addiction, sex, and violence, and I eventually found myself homeless, hopeless, and at the end of everything I had been chasing.

02 / My story

OUT OF ADDICTION.
INTO A NEW CHAPTER.

15

years ago,
my story took a different turn.

One of the first Bible verses God drew my attention to during my time at Adult & Teen Challenge was Acts 4:12:

“There is salvation in no one else, for there is no other name under heaven given to men by which we must be saved.”

Acts 4:12

Those words rang like a bell in my mind and heart because I had been searching for meaning, worth, and peace in everything else under heaven and had found none of it. God was offering me what I had needed all along—a relationship with Him—and that day I committed my life to the Lord. I’ve been serving Him ever since, imperfectly but passionately.

03 / Our family

A FAMILY.
A NEW CHAPTER.

Today, I get to share my life with my beautiful wife, Courtney, and our three children, Hannah, Solomon, and Valentino. They are my greatest accomplishment, and when I think about where I came from and the husband and father I’ve been able to become, I know it is only by the grace of God.

Along the way, I spent six years pastoring at Reclamation Church in Monroeville, and I now serve as Executive Director with Pennsylvania Adult & Teen Challenge and Naaman Network. The ministry where God met me and transformed my life fifteen years ago is now where I get to offer other men the same hope that was offered to me, often in circumstances much like the ones I once faced.

04 / Behind the mark

412 IS PITTSBURGH.
IT’S ALSO PERSONAL.

Through Just412, I want to share that hope with others and create products that draw our attention away from ourselves and toward Christ. No matter how far someone has fallen, I want them to know that there is redemption, there is meaning, and there is hope. If you’re in a place where that feels hard to believe, take it from me: I’ve been there, and it was there that God met me.

Grind by Grace,
Justin
